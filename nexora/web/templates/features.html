Welcome and roles

Welcome and goodbye messages with banners and buttons, auto roles, button, dropdown and reaction role menus, and temporary roles.

Community tools

Suggestions with voting and staff replies, polls, starboard, counting, birthdays, reminders and live member-count channels.

Custom commands

Create text or embed responses with placeholders, role restrictions and usage counts.

Scheduled announcements

Send a message or embed once or on repeat, managed from the dashboard.

AI assistant

An optional /ask command powered by Claude, limited to the channels and roles you choose. Needs your own API key.

Backups and diagnostics

Export and import a server's whole configuration, back up and restore the database, and watch errors and performance.
